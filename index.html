Efficient Open Language Models

We are building with open, efficient AI language models chosen for their performance on Arabic and classical text — keeping our process transparent and avoiding dependence on any single proprietary provider.

📚

Human & Scholarly Review

All AI-generated translations will undergo human review and, for religious or jurisprudential content, review by qualified Islamic scholars. We do not claim religious authority; translations are AI-assisted drafts that will be reviewed before being presented as reliable.

🌍

Free Access — Planned for Learners

We plan to make translated content freely available, particularly for students and learners who cannot afford paid resources, as part of our broader commitment to open access.

Note: This project is in early development. No translations have been published yet. All future content will be clearly labelled as AI-assisted and will carry reviewer attribution where applicable.

How We Work

From manuscript
to reviewed translation

A careful pipeline that combines the speed of AI with the precision of human and scholarly expertise.

1

Digitised Text In

Classical Arabic source texts are digitised and prepared, preserving original structure, diacritics, and scholarly annotations ready for processing.

2

AI Translation & Automated Checks

Multiple language model outputs are generated and compared. Automated checks flag inconsistencies in terminology, grammar, and meaning before human review.

3

Human Review & Publishing

Qualified reviewers — and scholars for religious content — assess, correct, and approve translations. Only reviewed content will be published and clearly attributed.

Roadmap

Planned phases

Our development is phased and honest — we will only claim progress when it has actually been made.

Phase 1

Arabic → English Translation

Building the core AI translation pipeline for Classical Arabic to English, with human and scholarly review, targeting key texts in Islamic scholarship.

Phase 2

Arabic → Urdu Translation

Expanding the pipeline to Urdu, serving South Asian scholars and learners and respecting the long tradition of Urdu Islamic scholarship.

Phase 3

Free AI Learning Resources & More Tools

Launching free and low-cost AI education content for underprivileged learners, alongside additional translation tools and further language pairs.
